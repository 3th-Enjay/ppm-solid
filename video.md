# Prompt Care Academy - Instructor Video Guide

This platform uses YouTube links for all video lessons. Instructors upload videos to YouTube and paste the link into the lesson editor.

## Step-by-step (Recommended)

1. Upload your video to YouTube.
2. Set visibility to **Public** or **Unlisted** (not Private).
3. Open the video and click **Share** to copy the link.
4. In the instructor dashboard, open:
   `My Courses` -> `Edit Content`
5. Add or edit a **Video** lesson and paste the link in **Video URL**.
6. Click **Save Content** to store the lesson changes.

## Supported YouTube link formats

- `https://youtu.be/VIDEO_ID`
- `https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VIDEO_ID`
- `https://www.youtube.com/embed/VIDEO_ID`
- `https://www.youtube.com/shorts/VIDEO_ID`

The `VIDEO_ID` is the 11-character YouTube video identifier.

## Important notes

- **Private videos will not play.** Use Public or Unlisted.
- Ensure **embedding is allowed** in YouTube settings.
- Do not paste channel links or playlist links into the Video URL field.

## Troubleshooting

- If the preview does not show:
  - Confirm the link is a valid YouTube video URL.
  - Ensure the video is not private.
  - Try opening the link in an incognito browser window.
  - Re-save the lesson after updating the link.
